# Product brief

Problem: Track quantity and date, then suggest recipes using soon-to-expire ingredients.

Audience: households, grocers and workplace kitchens

## First release

One user role, one core record (food-items) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Do not infer food safety from date alone; distinguish use-by from best-before and handle allergens.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
